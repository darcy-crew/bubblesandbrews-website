Our story

The woman behind the wheel

Cindy Luterbach didn't set out to build a mobile bar company. She set out to make celebrations feel like an event — and a fully restored vintage Piaggio Ape, pouring two drinks at a time at someone's wedding, turned out to be exactly that.

Cindy is a mother to five, an entrepreneur at heart, and the founder of Bubbles and Brews Canada. Alongside the Ape, she also runs BOHO Luxury Picnics — but ask her, and she'll tell you the best part of her week is still the one spent setting up florals on the truck before a Saturday wedding.

When she's not on-site pouring prosecco, Cindy's usually painting, sketching, or making something with her hands — the same instinct for detail that shows up in every dried-flower arrangement and hand-lettered sign you'll see at a Bubbles and Brews event.

Bubbles and Brews Canada has been pouring since 2020 — through every kind of celebration Calgary has to offer. Weddings, conventions, corporate events, second birthday parties, even a movie night in the park. If there's a reason to gather, there's a good chance the Ape has shown up for it.

Since then, Bubbles and Brews Canada has poured at 60+ events — weddings, engagements, corporate functions, conventions, birthday parties, and backyard celebrations across Calgary, Banff, and Canmore — and it's still Cindy, more often than not, behind the taps.
