What it is

A commercial 3-bowl frozen bar, without the bar staff.

The Frozen Bar is a full-size commercial slush machine with three 15-litre bowls, delivered to your event on a stainless steel table, pre-loaded with your chosen flavours, and ready for guests to serve themselves.

Each bowl holds enough for 50–60 drinks. All three together comfortably serves 90–120 guests at a 4-hour event. Mixes, cups, and straws are included — just add your own spirit at the tap, or run it entirely alcohol-free.

Dry-hire, consistent with all Bubbles & Brews services — you supply the alcohol, and we handle the rest. We'll tell you exactly how much to have on hand.

Booking & deposit

50% booking deposit secures your date. Balance due 7 days before your event. A $400 refundable damage deposit is held and returned within 5 business days of pickup, subject to inspection.

Site requirements

Any standard 110V outlet within reach. Level, hard surface for the machine. Mixes need to be refrigerated on arrival — we coordinate delivery timing to guarantee your 2-hour freeze window before guests arrive.
