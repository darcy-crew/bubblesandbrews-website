The dry-hire model

You provide the alcohol. We handle absolutely everything else.

Bubbles and Brews Canada operates dry-hire: you supply the alcohol, and we bring the truck, the bartenders, the taps, the drinkware, the ice, and the show. No cash bars, no liquor markup — your liquor cost stays exactly what you paid for it.

Alcohol must be on-site at the time of drop-off. We'll walk you through exactly what to buy and how much, based on your guest count and drink choices.

Booking & deposit

A $500 non-refundable deposit secures your date upon signed contract. The remaining balance is due 30 days prior to your event.

Power on site

A standard 110V, 15-amp outlet is required at the location of the cart. Power access and any related cost is the client's responsibility — we're happy to talk through this ahead of time.
